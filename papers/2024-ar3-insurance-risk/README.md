# Research on Insurance Industry Based on AR3 Multi Risk Model

**Transactions on Economics, Business and Management Research, 2024 · Co-first authors: Junjia Cao, Zhuodong Liu, and Xi Lei**

[DOI: 10.62051/e60z8783](https://doi.org/10.62051/e60z8783) · Volume 9, Issue 1, Pages 342–351 · [Paper PDF](paper.pdf)

## Overview

This paper develops two related models for disaster-insurance decision support: the **AR3 Multi-Hazard Insurance Model** for evaluating regional disaster-response capacity and the **Climate Risk Insurance Model (CRIM)** for forecasting insurance costs and claims.

## Method

- **AR3 indicators:** resilience, recovery, and adaptability, with secondary and tertiary measures such as Insurance Penetration Rate.
- **E-AHP:** computes indicator weights for regional evaluation.
- **ARIMA:** forecasts insurance costs and claims over a ten-year horizon.
- **Case studies:** California, USA, and Queensland, Australia.

## Reported Results

The paper reports that holding the analysed policies is unfeasible in **California from 2029 to 2033**, and in **Queensland in 2028 and from 2031 to 2033**. These are model-derived findings reported by the authors, not independent forecasts regenerated for this repository.
