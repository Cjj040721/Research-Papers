# Models to Mitigate the Effects of Drought on Plant Species

**2023 MCM/ICM report · Team 2302264, including Junjia Cao**

## Overview

This English-language modelling report examines how drought affects plant vitality, species richness, population size, and community benefits. It combines ecological modelling with optimisation and regression to explore drought adaptation.

## Method

- **Lotka–Volterra equations** for multi-species competition and biomass prediction.
- **Genetic algorithms** for estimating the effect of species count on forest benefits.
- **Multiple linear regression** for drought-related factors.
- **Logistic models** for comparing habitat loss and pollution scenarios.

## Reported Findings

The report describes a positive relationship between species richness and short-term drought resistance, with benefits leveling off near environmental carrying capacity. It also discusses threshold effects and the limits of using a single positive-correlation interpretation for long-term ecological management.

These findings are summarised from the original report and have not been independently reproduced for this repository.
